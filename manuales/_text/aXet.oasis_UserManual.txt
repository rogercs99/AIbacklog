In case of any support or request you can send an email for the following address: oasis.support.team@emeal.nttdata.com . aXet.oasis - What is Retrieval - Augmented Generation (RAG) and How to Use It Understanding RAG Retrieval - Augmented Generation (RAG) is a powerful method that enhances large language models (LLMs) by providing them with specific, targeted data to improve
their responses. Instead of relying solely on the LLM’s pre - trained information, RAG allows you to upload your own documents, which the model uses as a source of knowledge. This approach ensures that the responses are more accurate, context - specific, and r elevant to the content you provided. In other words, by using RAG, you create a personalized "knowledge base" that the LLM consults to answer your queries. This is especially useful when the information you need is detailed, niche, or frequently updated — beyond what the language model might already know. When you activate RAG on aXet.oasis , the system does two things: 1. Combines its built - in intelligence and preconfigured settings (Parameters and open files). 2. Utilizes additional data from the custom - made knowledge base you upload. The result? More precise and context - aware answers tailored to your specific needs. When Should You Use RAG? • When LLM Answers Need Specific Tailored Information : Use RAG when a standard language model’s response might not include nuanced or detailed knowledge specific to your area. • For Sensitive or Specialist Data : Incorporate RAG when you’re working with proprietary or specialist content that the AI won’t know otherwise. • To Keep Context Relevant : RAG ensures that your queries focus on a specific context relevant to your uploaded data, limiting unnecessary or overly broad answers. How Does RAG Work? 1. Document Upload : You start by uploading documents to the RAG system to your project. These could be files like text files, PDFs, Word documents (DOCX), or PowerPoint presentations (PPT), though only the text content will be extracted to train the AI. For best results, do cuments must be well - structured and concise. The documents uploaded are available to all users in the project to use in the RAG. 2. Indexing : Once uploaded, the documents are processed and indexed. This is the step where the system organizes the content to make it searchable and easier to retrieve when needed.
3. Context Building : During interactions with the AI, turn on the RAG mode to enable the RAG system to dynamically search your uploaded documents for relevant sections related to your queries. It includes only the necessary fragments in its analysis, ensuring the response al igns with the specific context. 4. Response Generation : The AI combines the retrieved fragments with its pre - trained knowledge to craft a complete and accurate response. After the answer is presented, the system provides references to show which parts of your uploaded documents were used. The documents are uploaded per project, so all affiliated users will be able to use them when RAG mode is enabled. How to Use RAG in the Application 1. Uploading Documents. • Go to the Context Management button on the homepage. • Select the RAG option from the dropdown menu. • In the Upload Documents area, use the drag - and - drop space or click to open a file selection window to choose the files to upload. Beware of the project initially selected in the Log - in page when uploading, as the documents will be made available for the entire project user list. • You can use the Cancel button to clear the entire list of files to upload or remove them individually as well from the list. • Click the Upload button to initiate the sending of the files to the RAG system. • Monitor the upload process in the progress bar. Any errors (e.g., unsupported file type or invalid size) will be listed. Tip: Use the "Document Guidelines" button for practical advice on structuring files for better performance during indexing and response generation. 2. Managing Uploaded Files • After clicking Upload, the sent files are displayed in the Document Overview table. • The table shows:
o File name, size, creator, upload date, indexing status, expiration countdown ( no automatic deletion after 30 days ), and a delete option . • Indexed document statuses include: o Pending (currently being indexed). o Indexed (ready to use with RAG). o Failed (an error occurred during indexing). • Users can sort table columns by name, size, upload date, or status, and search files by name for easier navigation. Tip: Use the Refresh button in the Documents Overview table to update the list and check the latest indexing status, especially if there are delays in the process. While the documents are being indexed you can Close the window and resume your work in the open sessions. 3. Using Uploaded Files in AI Conversations • Once files are indexed, activate the RAG Context toggle in your ChatAI session.
• With RAG active: o The AI responds to your questions by referencing your uploaded files. o Relevant documents are intelligently identified and used to craft responses. o Fragments of the documents used are shown below the response. Clicking on references allows you to view the exact text in a pop - up window. Important: If you have images open in the editor, they will not contribute to the response generation. You’ll receive a warning to notify you of this restriction.
4. Removing or Managing Files • Files can be deleted individually from the Documents Overview table, at any status of indexation. • Remember: Files uploaded will remain available and are not subject to automatic deletion after 30 days. 5. Optimizing Documents for Best Results To get the best responses from the AI, ensure your documents are: o Well - structured, with clear sections, headers, and concise language. o Relevant to the context you’ll be discussing with the AI. o Free of unnecessary or redundant information that might confuse the model.

aXet.oasis – Glossary This is a glossary of relevant terms: • Assisted Mode . An option for assisted chat. When assisted chat is turned on the models will continuously try to solve the provided problem. They will chat between each other. You can pause their conversation and send a message yourself. While the conversation is going you cannot edit the opened files, co ntext parameters, and the roles. • Capacity bar . Displays how much of the model’s capacity is currently occupied by the opened files, context parameters and chat history. This is only updated after a reply is received. If you try to send more information than the model can handle you will get an error. • C ontext Parameters . The main way for you to configure what aXet.oasis knows and how it behaves. They inform the model on the project and allow you to create rules for the assistant to follow. • Deep Learning . Deep Learning is a type of Machine Learning that uses complex neural networks with three or more layers. These neural networks attempt to emulate the behavior of the human brain — though far from matching its capacity — but allow it to "learn" from large amo unts of data. While a single - layer neural network can make approximate predictions, additional hidden layers add complexity to the model and help optimize its accuracy. • Fine - Tuning . Fine - Tuning is a training technique that involves reusing predefined and pre - trained neural network architectures. It is a process where a "fine - tuning" of some layers of the network is performed to obtain the desired outputs. In other words, certain rep resentations of the pre - trained model are slightly adjusted to make it more relevant to the problem to be solved. This avoids training the network from scratch. • Generative AI . Generative Artificial Intelligence is a field of Artificial Intelligence that encompasses systems and algorithms capable of generating new, original, and creative content. This type of AI learns from enormous datasets and generates new similar content (t ext, images, music, and other types of data). It falls within a specific type of Machine Learning known as Deep Learning, which is based on the use of deep neural networks. These networks are configured in various architectures, such as Genera tive Adversarial Networks (GANs) or diffusion models based on Transformers.
• LM and LLM – Large Language Models . Language models (LM) are machine learning models that predict the next word in a sentence, considering the context of previous words. They are based on a probabilistic description of language phenomena. A colossal language model or LLM, also called a lar ge language model, is a language model consisting of a neural network with many parameters, trained on large amounts of unlabeled text through self - supervised or semi - supervised learning. These models can be used in a wide range of natural language processing tasks, such as text creation, document summarization, or language translation. • Machine Learning . Machine Learning is a branch of computer science that aims to enable computers to perform tasks without being explicitly programmed to do so. These are models that can gradually improve their accuracy based on the data provided. Its most basic applicatio n is the practice of using algorithms to process data, learn from it, and then be able to make a prediction or suggestion about something. It comprises three categories of learning: supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. • Neural Networks . Neural networks are a type of computational model that teaches machines to process data in a way inspired by the human brain. It consists of a set of units, called artificial neurons, connected to transmit signals. The input information passes through th e neural network (where it undergoes various operations), producing output values. • NLP . Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field within artificial intelligence and applied linguistics that studies interactions using natural language between humans and machines. • Prompts . A "prompt" in AI refers to an instruction or request that a user makes to an LLM, chatbot, or AI model. This instruction can come not only in natural language (text or audio) but also in other formats like images or code. The careful and concise preparat ion of these instructions is known as "prompting," and it ultimately aims to obtain the desired results or responses from LLMs or generative models of images and videos. • Provider . The provider is the company that gives us access to their LLM’s, this is a ‘model as a service’ paradigm, where the provider creates the model and uses their hardware to run the inference, and we use the model paying only for the tokens consumed and gene rated. In aXet.oasis you have several providers and models available. • RAG . Retrieval - Augmented Generation (RAG) is a methodology to optimize LLMs so that they take into account a reference knowledge base when generating responses. This way, with RAG, we augment LLMs' capabilities with internal, more current, or specific data. • Reinforcement Learning . Reinforcement Learning is a type of learning in ML models where the machine is guided to learn from the repetition of actions and associated rewards. The agent, representing the machine, takes actions in an environment and receives rewards when correct d ecisions are made. Over time, the agent learns to choose the correct actions to maximize its reward. Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback is the technique behind large language models like asistente conversacional. • Supervised Learning . Supervised Learning is a type of learning in Machine Learning models that relies on labeled data and has high accuracy and easily interpretable results, which is why it is widely used in industry. Its main drawback is that it requires data to be previous ly labeled (which can be costly or unfeasible in many cases). • Token . In the context of large language models or LLMs, a token is a basic unit of information for text processing. These tokens generally correspond to words or phrases, but they can also be punctuation marks or numbers. Transforming text into chains of tokens allows LLMs to process user prompts and generate corresponding responses. A model like could handle 4,096 tokens or around 8,000 words. The number of tokens processed in an input grows as more complex LLMs are trained. • Tools/Auto CRUD An option to enable/disable the tools. It is a neural network architecture that has served as the basis for deep learning models used in modern generative AI models. Transformers specialize in processing sequential information, such as natural language, and for this, they employ the self - atte ntion mechanism, where different weights are given to each part of the input. These models take a sentence in the form of a sequence of vectors, convert it into an encoded vector, which is then decoded into an output sequence. This architecture was proposed by a group of Google researchers in 2017, in the seminal paper "Attention is all you need." • Unsupervised Learning . Unsupervised Learning is a type of learning in ML models that uses unlabeled data and, unlike Supervised Learning models, can detect hidden patterns in the data without human intervention. This includes clustering algorithms, for example, to group custom ers into segments and detect similar behaviors.
